Privacy Policy — Humivane Uploader

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Humivane Uploader is a private automation tool used by the owner of the "Humivane" YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@Humivane) to upload and schedule that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What the app accesses: YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload only, under the channel owner's own Google account, after the owner signs in and approves access.

What data is stored: OAuth access/refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer solely so scheduled uploads can run. No other personal data is collected, stored, or processed.

What is shared: Nothing. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. The app communicates only with Google's APIs.

Revoking access: The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

Contact: Channel owner, via the Humivane YouTube channel page linked above.
